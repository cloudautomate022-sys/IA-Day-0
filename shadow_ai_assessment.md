# 🗂️ Diagnostic Discovery Questions (Identifying Shadow AI & Structural Risks)

Use these calibrated questions during your first discovery calls or alignment meetings with C-Level clients. They are designed to shift the CEO's perspective from technical fear to strategic data management.

### 1. Policy & Workflow Boundaries
* *"Do you currently have any endpoint blocks or network restrictions implemented for public AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude?"*
  * **Objective:** If they say "yes, we block them," you can immediately pivot to the data showing that 48% of their staff bypasses this rule on personal networks, creating an unmonitored security vacuum.
* *"If an employee needs to draft an urgent proposal or summarize a 50-page technical report on a Sunday afternoon, what official corporate AI tool do they have access to?"*
  * **Objective:** Highlight that a lack of secure enterprise tools is the root cause of personal account usage.

### 2. Legacy Permissions & Data Inheritance
* *"How often does your IT department audit application permissions for human users? Are you certain that old, unused API keys aren't active and vulnerable to inheritance by new AI agents?"*
  * **Objective:** Expose the structural risk: 96% of enterprise app tokens sit idle but active, creating data access points that corporate AI plugins automatically inherit if not properly governed.
* *"If we ran an anonymous internal survey today, what percentage of your operational staff do you honestly think would admit to using personal AI to get their daily tasks done faster?"*
  * **Objective:** Force the executive to confront reality—employees will prioritize efficiency over corporate restrictions.

### 3. Financial & Executive Focus
* *"Is your company’s current technology policy optimized to eliminate short-term security blame, or is it architected to capture the 1.7x growth speed that your AI-First competitors are achieving?"*
  * **Objective:** Reposition AI from an "IT ticket/system issue" into a core executive business metric focused on revenue and margins.
